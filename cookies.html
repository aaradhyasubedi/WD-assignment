Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla essence
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips
  • A pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix butter, sugar and brown sugar in a bowl.
  3. Add the egg and vanilla essence and mix well.
  4. Add flour, baking soda and salt.
  5. Mix everything to make a soft dough.
  6. Add chocolate chips and mix gently.
  7. Make small round balls from the dough.
  8. Place them on a baking tray with some space between them.
  9. Bake for 10-12 minutes until golden brown.
  10. Allow the cookies to cool before serving.
