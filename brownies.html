Chocolate Brownies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla essence
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • A pinch of salt
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Take a bowl and mix melted butter and sugar.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla essence and mix well.
  4. Add flour, cocoa powder, baking powder and salt.
  5. Mix everything until you get a smooth batter.
  6. Add chocolate chips and stir gently.
  7. Pour the batter into a greased baking tray.
  8. Bake for 20-25 minutes.
  9. Allow the brownies to cool before cutting them into pieces.
