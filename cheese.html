Grilled Cheese Sandwich

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2 slices of cheese
  • 1 tablespoon of butter
  • A pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Take two slices of bread.
  2. Place the cheese between the two slices of bread.
  3. Spread butter on the outer sides of the bread.
  4. Heat a pan on medium heat.
  5. Place the sandwich in the pan and cook until golden brown.
  6. Flip the sandwich and cook the other side until the cheese melts.
  7. Remove from the pan, cut into two halves and serve warm.
