🥐 Homemade Croissants

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon yeast
  • 3/4 cup warm milk
  • 1 cup cold butter
  • 1 egg for brushing

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, sugar, salt, yeast and warm milk in a bowl.
  2. Knead the mixture to make a soft dough.
  3. Cover the dough and let it rest until it rises.
  4. Roll the dough into a rectangle.
  5. Place cold butter on the dough and fold it several times.
  6. Roll the dough and cut it into triangles.
  7. Roll each triangle into a croissant shape.
  8. Brush the croissants with beaten egg.
  9. Bake at 200°C for 15-20 minutes until golden brown.

Enjoy Your Croissants!

Your homemade croissants are ready! Enjoy them warm with tea, coffee or milk.
