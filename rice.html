Chicken Fried Rice

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 cup boneless chicken
  • 1 egg
  • 1 small onion (chopped)
  • 1/2 cup chopped carrots
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper as needed
  • Spring onions for decoration

Preparation Method

  1. First, wash and cut the chicken into small pieces.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and cook the chicken until fully cooked.
  3. Add chopped onions, carrots and green peas. Cook for a few minutes.
  4. Beat the egg and add it to the pan. Mix everything well.
  5. Add the cooked rice and mix it properly with the vegetables and chicken.
  6. Add soy sauce, salt and pepper. Stir everything together.
  7. Cook for another 2-3 minutes and turn off the heat.
  8. Garnish with chopped spring onions and serve hot.
